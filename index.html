Ok, you want free stuff? Want to get noticed in the skateboard industry? Well, here's your chance.

Send your video to:

videos@bestnobody.com

DropBox

Please do not submit videos that have previously been submitted elsewhere. This includes YouTube. We only accept original footage without music. If you have a song you would like to use with your part, we'll work with you but please send separately.

Thanks to our sponsors, we have been fortunate enough to give away product to the best talent out there. With the increase of online skate shops, there has been a decrease in shop sponsors. We are trying to help fill that void. Obviously, we can't afford giving stuff away gear for your first kickflip on your driveway so we have to be selective. (Hint: originality, speed, style, tech, big, are all bonuses. sketchy, slow, ugly are no good.) And sorry, but once you send us those videos and music, it's ours. We can't afford to ship DVDs and USB cards all over the world so please make yourself a copy.

Skaters: If we don't use your footage, don't cry. It might be a while till we get to it since so many people have sent us hours of garbage. Keep skating for fun and see if your local shop sponsors. If you do get featured, we will follow up with you to get your board, wheels, and clothes size via email. We will also need a local skate shop for you to pick up your package. Best Nobody merchandise is exclusive for select riders and NOT for resale. If you are good enough to get one of our Best Nobody shirts and are featured in a Best Nobody video wearing it, we will send you a package containing a mystery product with mystery instructions. Mysterious, huh? Get footy of yourself featured wearing our select sponsor's gear, get a Best Nobody t-shirt.

Retailers: We sponsor you too! Carry any of our select sponsors, and we'll feature your store in a commercial or photos on our website. You will also be eligible for special in-store only items from our sponsors.

Companies: We accept any and all donations. Anything from a sticker to throw away footage is much appreciated. All we can offer is in return is advertisement space and introduce you to some up and coming rippers. Please contact us for more details.

Everyone else: If your local skate shop starts a new account with one of our select sponsors, we will put you on our flow list. That means you get free stuff or maybe even money? For more details, send email to info@bestnobody.com

Please help support this skateboard movement by liking us on our facebook fanpage. http://www.facebook.com/BestNobody
